The maintainer requested a stricter independent review after the final Three.js dashboard, landing-page architecture and Korean explanation pass. Review the immutable commit supplied in the task message, not an unqualified moving working tree. Each of the four GPT-6.1 Sol Max teams writes its own `final-teamN.json`; the main agent owns dispositions and fixes.

Read the exact criterion descriptors in `docs/hackathon/rubric-baseline.json`, the staged acceptance gates, the product contract and the existing ledger. The organizer PDF is the official source; the local planning and acceptance targets are separate. No official weights, 85-point total, N/A policy or award probability is established.

## Required assessment

1. Identify the reviewed commit and exact evidence inspected. Verify that current runtime files match the capture's hashes, or explicitly assess a bounded source delta. Historical failures and captures remain historical.
2. Assess each owned criterion against implemented, verified and captured evidence. Distinguish code quality from publication, interview or public-discussion evidence that has not occurred.
3. Give explicit verdicts for the relevant internal stage gates. A missing required outcome is not waived by a good-looking page or a reviewed optional-feature deferral.
4. Recheck earlier findings assigned to the team. State resolved, partially resolved or unresolved with evidence; do not close a finding only because its author reports completion.
5. Inspect the final dashboard/pitch/video/article and Korean guide for claim consistency. The 3D explorer's nodes and links must correspond to actual code/resource structures; logical modules are not separate deployed processes. Secure single-installation and distributed HTTP lab profiles are alternatives.
6. For every actionable finding provide criterion/gate IDs, observed source/evidence, impact, smallest reasonable correction, product justification and verification method. Separate release blockers from optional future improvements. Strictness means demanding evidence for current claims, not inventing unrelated requirements.
