Date: 18 November 2026 (KST). This is an internal evidence map, not official scoring. The original handout defines 17 criteria and performance bands, but does not establish an official total, weights, track applicability or an N/A rule. No winning probability is inferred.

Four independent GPT-6.1 Sol Max teams reviewed immutable implementation commit `2b2a239`. Their corrections are tracked against `fd5e648`. Final combined-release verification remains separate. Exact original names/descriptors and source identity are in `rubric-baseline.json`.

The strongest demonstrated technical areas are native oneM2M common services, protocol diversity, a precise SDT subset, verified action outcomes and reusable agent tooling. The largest scoring uncertainties are the organizer's interpretation of complexity, the five unimplemented advanced edge criteria, actual public deliverable availability and authentic interview/discussion performance. These gaps are visible rather than converted into invented points.
