What the labels mean

Virtual facility: equipment and physical readings are simulated. The facility drawing illustrates relationships; it is not an airflow calculation or a certified ventilation assessment.

oneM2M connected: the service reports a reachable CSE. This is separate from a successful command, device acknowledgement or recovery.

Deterministic run / live model: the execution label comes from the recorded run. A scripted scenario is not labeled as a live LLM decision.
